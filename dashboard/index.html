💡 Strategic Insights & Data-Backed HR Recommendations

🚨 Key Turnover Risk Drivers

  • Compensation Disparity: Employees earning <$3,000/month face a 68.8% turnover rate versus 7.1% for staff earning >$15,000.
  • Early Tenure Vulnerability: 78.4% of total company exits happen within 0-2 years of joining (51.2% attrition rate).
  • Overtime Burnout: Overtime workers experience 54.1% turnover compared to 32.8% for non-overtime peers.
  • High-Risk Roles: Laboratory Technicians (63.3%) and Sales Representatives (55.4%) face the highest exit rates.

🎯 Recommended Action Plan

  • Revise Entry-Level Pay: Benchmark & increase baseline salary floor for Lab Techs & Sales Reps to eliminate compensation-driven exits.
  • 90-Day Retention Mentorship: Implement mandatory early-stage onboarding check-ins and peer mentors for 0-2 year staff.
  • Overtime Workload Caps: Enforce strict weekly overtime limits and redistribute excess workload to audit staffing.
  • Transparent Career Paths: Establish clear 2-year promotion roadmaps so entry-level staff see growth potential.
